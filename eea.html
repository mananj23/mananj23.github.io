Leadership · Electrical Engineering Association · IIT Kanpur · 2025–26

A year of building the EE community

As one of six Heads of the Electrical Engineering Association, I helped run 20 initiatives across the year, from the first-week orientation to the graduating batch’s farewell. My own focus was research access: I built the Research Portal that connects students with professors’ projects.

20initiatives in one academic year
28people on the team: 6 Heads, 22 secretaries
500+applications for Winter Projects
5institutes’ researchers at the IEEE VLSI symposium

Month by month

Highlighted cards are the ones I built or led myself. Everything else was a team effort across the association.

  1. August – September 2025

    Secretary recruitment

    Onboarded the team that would run the year: 22 secretaries working with 6 Heads.

    Department orientation

    Walked the incoming Y25 batch through the curriculum, opportunities and the society, with a Research Wing overview for Y25 and Y24.

    9 Sep 2025
    “Ask Your Seniors” mid-sem mentorship

    Y24 students met top Y23 performers for course-specific strategy and doubt-solving before their first departmental mid-sems.

    11 Sep 2025

  2. October 2025

    Internship guidance reel series

    Recorded Y23 students’ preparation strategies and lessons, and cut them into short reels for juniors with little guidance.

    Departmental Freshers

    A masquerade-themed welcome for Y25 with performances, games and the Mr. and Ms. EE ’25 competition.

    31 Oct 2025

  3. November 2025

    EEA Research Portal

    A single place to discover undergraduate research in the department. Prof. Tushar Sandhan’s projects for the next semester were released exclusively through it.

    Interactive alumni session

    Two alumni spoke openly about higher studies, placements and life after IIT Kanpur.

    2 Nov 2025
    Volunteer recruitment

    Brought Y25 students into the department community through hands-on event work.

    Winter Projects: curation and selection

    Worked with professors to curate 11 projects (6 for EE, 5 campus-wide), picked mentors, and ran a selection test that drew 500+ responses.

    IEEE Symposium on Microelectronics and VLSI

    With IEEE, brought speakers from IIT Hyderabad, IIT Patna, IISc Bengaluru, IIT Madras and IIT Jodhpur to campus.

    29 Nov 2025

  4. December 2025 – January 2026

    Winter Projects

    Hands-on projects in control systems, communication, electronics and ML, with a kickoff and weekly check-ins through secretary points of contact.

    Conference participation initiative

    With the department, helped students attend ICEE 2025, MAPCON 2025, IWPSD 2025 and the VLSI Design conference, with support for registration and travel.

    Placement insights blog series

    Turned Y22 students’ placement experiences into structured blog posts for juniors across departments.

    Resume building session

    I co-led this session with Soham Panchal: how to structure a resume, choose what to highlight, and avoid common mistakes.

    5 Jan 2026

  5. February – March 2026

    Lab tours

    Opened the department’s research labs to first- and second-year students to make research approachable before SURGE.

    6–7 Feb 2026
    Department merchandise

    Designed and released official EE T-shirts, with discounts for members, and hoodies for the EEA team.

    Happy Hour

    Games and informal conversation between students and faculty, outside the classroom.

    27 Mar 2026

  6. April – May 2026

    Signal Sprint hackathon

    A 15-day, multi-phase hackathon with Prof. Tushar Sandhan on overflowing dustbins: data collection, analysis, ML models, an app, and final pitches. Top 20 teams won prizes.

    Apr 2026
    Department farewell

    Faculty addresses, performances and custom jackets for the graduating Class of 2026.

    12 Apr 2026
    Graduating batch photoshoot

    The official photoshoot for the Class of 2026.

    10 May 2026

What I took from it

  • Build the system, not just the event. The Research Portal and the Winter Projects pipeline keep working after one batch of organisers leaves.
  • Close information gaps early. Most of our best-attended work, from orientation to the reel series, answered questions students did not know whom to ask.
  • Work across boundaries. Professors, alumni, IEEE and five other institutes all had a part in this year.
